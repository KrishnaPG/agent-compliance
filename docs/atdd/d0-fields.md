# d0 field names

The only field paths the packet, the result JSON, its jobs and the config may have, as the owner decided them for d0 (2026-11-25). Generated by `docs/atdd/build.py` from `docs/atdd/fields.yaml`; do not edit. Acceptance rows in `docs/atdd/criteria/` may read no other path; `build.py --check` fails on one.
